1. What ResiboScan does

ResiboScan is an Android application designed to help you record, organize, and manage GCash payment receipts.

The app allows you to:

  • Photograph or import receipt images.
  • Automatically recognize receipt details using on-device text recognition.
  • Review, edit, and save receipt records.
  • Organize and search your receipt history.
  • Identify possible duplicate receipts.
  • Export selected receipt records as CSV files and email them to yourself.
  • Access additional features through a Pro subscription.

ResiboScan is an independent application. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by GCash or Mynt.

ResiboScan does not connect to your GCash account, process payments, transfer money, or independently verify whether a transaction has been completed.

2. Accuracy and use of receipt information

ResiboScan uses automated text recognition to read information from receipt images. This process may occasionally produce incorrect or incomplete results, especially when receipts are blurry, cropped, damaged, or formatted differently.

You are responsible for reviewing and verifying receipt information before saving, exporting, or relying on it.

Please check important details such as:

  • Transaction amount.
  • Sender name.
  • Reference number.
  • Transaction date and time.

Duplicate detection is provided as a convenience and may not identify every duplicate or may occasionally flag records incorrectly.

ResiboScan is a record-keeping tool and does not serve as official proof of payment, financial advice, or independent verification of GCash transactions.

You should always refer to your original receipt or official transaction records when verifying a payment.
