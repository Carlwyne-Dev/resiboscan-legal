ResiboScan

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

The short version

Your receipts and receipt photos stay on your phone. Receipt scanning and text recognition also happen on your device.

You can use ResiboScan without creating an account. If you create an account, we collect your email address, a securely hashed version of your PIN, and your subscription information so you can access account features, export receipts, and use Pro.

When you request an export, only the receipt records you select are sent to our server to generate a CSV file and email it to your verified email address. We do not use your receipt information for advertising or analytics.

We do not sell your personal information.

2. What stays on your phone

You can use ResiboScan without creating an account.

The following information is stored locally in the app's private storage and is not uploaded to our servers during normal app use:

  • Receipt records: Amount, sender name, reference number, date and time, and any notes you add.
  • Receipt photos: Copies of photos you take or select when adding receipts. The original photos in your gallery are not modified or deleted.
  • Personal settings: The name you choose for the greeting, app preferences, and your free scan count.

ResiboScan uses Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition to read information from receipt photos. Receipt images are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers for text recognition.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, locally stored records and receipt photos may be permanently deleted. We cannot recover them if they have not been exported.

We recommend exporting important records regularly.

4. When you export receipts

When you request an export, ResiboScan sends the receipt records within the date range you selected to our server through an encrypted connection.

Receipt photos are not included in the export.

Our server processes the selected records to generate a CSV file and sends it to your verified account email address.

The receipt records and generated CSV file are not intended to be permanently stored on our server after the export has been processed and sent. We retain only the export history described in Section 3.

Our hosting and email providers may temporarily process information as necessary to generate and deliver your export. Once delivered, the email and its attachment may remain in your email inbox and be subject to your email provider's storage and retention practices.

If an export fails, temporary files and processing data are handled according to our applicable server retention and deletion procedures.

8. How long we keep your information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Our general retention practices are:

  • Account information: Retained while your account remains active, until you request account deletion.
  • Export history: Retained while your account remains active, until account deletion.
  • Verification and reset codes: Expire after 10 minutes.
  • Local receipt records and photos: Remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, uninstall the app, or delete your account through the app.
  • Temporary export files: Intended to be deleted after export processing is completed.

Certain information may remain temporarily in backups or security logs according to applicable retention schedules, or may be retained when required by law.

9. Deleting your account

You may request account deletion through the following methods:

Through the app:

Go to Settings → Delete Account and confirm your request using your PIN.

When deletion is completed through the app, your account information, email address, PIN, subscription status, and export history will be deleted from our active systems. Locally stored receipt records and photo copies in ResiboScan on that device will also be deleted.

Original photos in your phone's gallery are not affected.

Through email:

If you cannot access the app, email resiboscan@gmail.com using your registered account email address with the subject line:

"Delete my account"

We will process your request within 30 days.

Requests made through email or our external account deletion webpage will delete your server-side account information. Since receipt records and photos are stored locally on your device, you may need to delete those separately if you still have access to the device.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or may be retained where required by law.

Deleting your ResiboScan account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. You must cancel your subscription separately through Google Play.

10. How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we process.

These include:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) when communicating with our servers.
  • Secure, one-way hashing of PINs and verification codes.
  • Temporary account lockouts and sign-in attempt limits.
  • Restricted access to server-side information and account records.
  • Appropriate security controls for our hosting infrastructure.

No system or method of electronic storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the event of a personal data breach, we will investigate, take appropriate steps to address the incident, and notify affected individuals and the appropriate authorities when required by applicable law.
