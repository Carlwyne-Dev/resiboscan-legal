Delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ResiboScan and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Enter your PIN and tap Delete my account.

Without the app

Email resiboscan@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and PIN.
  • Your plan, subscription status and export history.
  • When deleted in the app, also the receipts and photo copies saved in ResiboScan on that phone. Photos in your gallery are not touched.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Receipt records and photos are never stored on our servers.

Have Pro?

Deleting your account doesn't cancel billing. Cancel your subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
